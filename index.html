Technology / 06

The movement behind it.

At the heart of every OG watch is a self-winding mechanical movement. No batteries, no quartz tick — just a mainspring, a rotor and 25 jewels keeping beat, second after second.

It is a 200-year-old idea, refined to its essentials. When you wear an OG, you are wearing a tiny machine that lives as long as you keep it moving.
